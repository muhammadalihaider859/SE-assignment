# E-Commerce System

## Overview

The **E-Commerce System** is a software system designed to help businesses sell products and services online. It allows customers to browse products, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries electronically.

This project is developed as part of the **Real World Software System** course.

## Users

The system is designed to support four main types of users:

- **Customers**
- **Sellers / Vendors**
- **Delivery Staff**
- **Admins**

## Stakeholders

The major stakeholders of the system include:

- Customers
- Sellers
- Payment Providers
- Logistics Partners
- Business Owners
- Government / Regulators

## Main Features

The system provides the following core functionalities:

### Product Search
Customers can search for products available on the platform.

### Cart Management
Customers can manage the products they want to purchase through a shopping cart.

### Checkout & Payment
Customers can proceed to checkout and make payments for their orders.

### Order Tracking
Customers can track the status and delivery of their orders.

### Inventory Management
Sellers can manage the inventory of their products.

## Non-Functional Requirements

The system should meet the following requirements:

- **Security** – Protect system and user information.
- **Performance** – Provide efficient system operations.
- **Scalability** – Support growth in users, products, and transactions.
- **Availability** – Keep the system accessible and operational.
- **Usability** – Provide an easy and convenient experience for users.

## Risks

Some potential risks associated with the E-Commerce System are:

- Payment fraud
- Server downtime
- Data privacy breaches

## Quality Attributes

The important quality attributes of the system are:

- **Reliability**
- **Security**
- **Scalability**

## AI Applications

AI can be used in different areas of the E-Commerce System, including:

- Product recommendations

### Risks of Incorrect AI Usage

Incorrect implementation or use of AI may result in:
